FMP: Paul Bérenger demands a new government team as soon as possible

Mise en ligne FMP7 novembre 2026 · 15:44 (Maurice)

Paul Bérenger is demanding the establishment of a new government team as soon as possible. In front of the press, this Saturday, November 7, the leader of the Fron Militan Progresis (FMP) has notably requested the appointment of a new Prime Minister.

Speaking of the files related to drugs and law enforcement, he also advocated for the appointment of a Deputy Prime Minister, a full-time Minister of Finance, and a Minister of the Interior in charge of police and drug enforcement.

For Paul Bérenger, "the sooner it comes, the better for the country."

The FMP leader has also deplored the announced absence of Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam at the parliamentary session next Tuesday, due to a trip to Paris.
